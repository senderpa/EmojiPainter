Emoji Painter is a super-simple art app for desktop & phones — like a kid-friendly Photoshop. Every day brings three topics. Pick a timer, paint within that time, and upload if you love it. This gallery lists everything by day & topic — always the newest first. Big date banners mark each new day as you scroll.

ℹ️ How topics & mystery paintings work

🗓️ Daily Topics. Every day the app serves three fresh topics, the same for everyone worldwide. You pick one, a sprint timer starts (a few minutes only!), you paint it with emoji brushes and — if you love the result — upload it here. The time pressure is the point: no overthinking, no polishing, just honest fast painting. The gallery sorts everything by day and topic, newest first.

🎲 Mystery Topics. The fourth challenge is different: you get a secret topic drawn from a pool of 999+ curated topics (plus community-suggested ones). You paint it so clearly that strangers can guess it — but in the gallery the topic stays hidden behind masked letters. Other players hit Guess, type their answer letter by letter, and when someone solves it the painting is revealed and the solver counts up. Painting a mystery is a test of how much meaning you can pack into emojis; guessing one is a test of how well you can read another human's encoding.

💡 Suggest topics. Tap the 💡 button up top to suggest your own topics and vote on others — approved suggestions join the mystery pool for everyone.

🛡️ The philosophy. Everything here is anti-AI image making. Each painting is made by a human, under time pressure, out of emojis — symbols that carry meaning in every language. That makes these images a human encoding: translatable but not scrapeable. An AI can generate pixels; it cannot fake hundreds of deliberate symbol choices racing a clock — and it cannot guess a mystery painting the way you can. Human eyes only. Read more →
