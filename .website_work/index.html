🛡️ Anti-AI Image Making

Every EmojiPainter artwork is painted by a human, stroke by stroke, out of living emojis. That makes each image a kind of encoding: every emoji carries its own meaning, history and emotion — so a painting is loaded with information that stays translatable across every language and culture, but can never be flattened into training data. AI image generators can imitate pixels — they cannot imitate a thousand deliberate, meaningful symbol choices made by a human hand under time pressure.

It is, in a way, an encrypted way to paint: readable by people, unscrapeable by machines. No prompts, no models, no fakes — just humans, emojis and a ticking clock.

A project by the maker of hittheai.com & sprayar.com — fighting for human-made images.

About EmojiPainter

EmojiPainter is a next-generation creative app that treats emojis as a universal, pixel-rich brush language. Whether you’re sketching a playful poster, composing animated loops, or exporting a gallery-grade print, the workflow is intentionally simple: choose emojis, paint on an infinite canvas, layer your composition, and export in the format you need. Because emojis are globally recognized, your artwork carries meaning across languages and cultures — perfect for a networked audience.

What is the Emoji Gallery?

The gallery is a living grid of community works, updated continuously. It includes featured pieces, daily-prompt highlights, and curated series. You can browse on desktop or phone, open artworks full-screen, and follow artists through their public links.

  • Real-time updates from the online painter
  • Curated front page with rotating themes
  • High-resolution detail views

How the Daily Prompts Work

Every day, a new topic is announced inside the online painter. When you pick the prompt, the canvas focuses and a subtle timer appears. You paint with any emoji; once you submit, your piece enters the stream and replays next day at the exact time it was created.

  • Fresh daily topic with gentle timeboxing
  • Automatic inclusion in the live gallery
  • Replay the next day at the same time

Why Artists Use EmojiPainter

Speed, clarity, and reach. Emojis are dense pictograms with color and emotion. In EmojiPainter you compose with thousands of them, then export for print, motion, or web. The app fits rapid ideation and exhibition-ready output in one place.

  • High-res PNG/JPEG, GIF & MP4 exports
  • Layered editing with blend modes
  • Optimized performance for big canvases
